MoneyBag Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

In short: MoneyBag has no accounts and no servers of its own. The money information you enter — accounts, transactions, budgets and goals — stays on your device and is never sent to us. Free users may see an optional rewarded video ad from Google, which collects some device and usage data as described below.

Information stored on your device

MoneyBag keeps the following on your device only, so the app can work:

  • Your accounts, balances, transactions, categories, budgets and savings goals.
  • Your settings (currency, whether Face ID lock is on) and your free-allowance counters.

We never receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it. If you back up your device with iCloud or your computer, Apple may include the app's data in that backup under Apple's own terms.

Importing from the earlier version

If you used the earlier version of MoneyBag, version 2.0 reads that app's local database on your device once and copies your accounts and transactions into the new app. Nothing leaves your device.

Face ID lock

If you turn on the optional app lock, MoneyBag asks iOS to verify you with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. This is handled entirely by Apple. MoneyBag never receives your biometric data.

Exporting your data

With MoneyBag Pro you can export your transactions to a CSV file. The file is created on your device and you choose where it goes (Files, AirDrop, Mail and so on). We don't receive it.

Purchases

MoneyBag Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and optional tips are purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and we never see your payment details. MoneyBag asks Apple whether your Apple ID owns a purchase so that unlocking and "Restore purchases" work. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Advertising (free users)

When you choose to watch a rewarded video for extra transactions, the ad is provided by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform). Google may collect these data from your device to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud:

DataUsed for
Device identifiersAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from IP address)Advertising, analytics, consent
Ad interactions and app usage (for example, ads viewed)Advertising, analytics
Diagnostics (crash, performance and other diagnostic data)Analytics, advertising

MoneyBag does not use the iOS advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) MoneyBag asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are shown.

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your financial information is never shared with advertisers. MoneyBag Pro users do not need to watch ads.

Children

MoneyBag is a personal-finance app intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Choose whether to watch rewarded videos at all, or buy Pro to avoid them.
  • Delete any account, transaction or category inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Control tracking permissions for all apps in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
