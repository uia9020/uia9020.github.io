Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored? Do I need an account?

There is no account. Your accounts, transactions, budgets and goals are stored on your device only and are never uploaded. If you delete the app, or erase or replace your phone without a backup, that data is gone, so keep your iPhone backed up. See the Privacy Policy.

How many transactions can I add for free?

30 new transactions every calendar month, resetting on the 1st. When you run out you can watch a short video for 10 more (up to 3 videos a day), or upgrade to MoneyBag Pro for no limit. You can always view, edit and delete your existing data for free.

What does MoneyBag Pro include?

Unlimited transactions, 6-month insights and trends, Face ID app lock, and CSV export. Choose monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open MoneyBag › More › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › MoneyBag, and cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I create my own categories?

Yes, and it's free. Tap New at the end of the category list when adding a transaction, or open More › Categories to add, edit or delete them. If you delete a category, its transactions move to "Other".

I used the older version of MoneyBag. Is my data still there?

Yes. The first time you open version 2.0 it imports your old accounts and transactions automatically. Old expenses appear under "Other" and old income under "Other income", and you can re-categorise them. They don't count toward your free monthly limit.

How do I change the currency?

Open More › Currency. This changes how amounts are displayed; it doesn't convert existing amounts.

How do I export my data?

With Pro, open More › Export to CSV and choose where to save or share the file.

The video ad didn't load
