Frequently asked questions

How many idioms can I see for free?

Four a day: the morning idiom plus three more. Watch a short video for three more, as often as you like. The count resets at midnight. Idioms Pro removes the limit and the ads.

I'm not getting the 8am idiom

Check iOS Settings › Notifications › Idioms For You and make sure notifications are allowed. Opening the app once refreshes the schedule for the next two weeks.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. All 1000 idioms are inside the app. You only need a connection for ads, purchases and restoring Pro.

How do I restore Idioms Pro on a new device?

Open the app, tap Go Pro, then Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Idioms Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. What you've seen and your favourites stay on your iPhone. The free version shows Google ads — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what Google collects.
